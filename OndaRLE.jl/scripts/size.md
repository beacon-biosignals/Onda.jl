# Onda hypnogram serialization: size & speed

## Notes

- `lpcm.rle` (varint counts) wins on size for typical hypnograms with long runs and dominates the fixed-width `u16` / `u32` variants in every scenario tested — both on size and on speed.
- When runs are short or absent (high-entropy data), `lpcm.zst` is more robust and RLE can actually inflate the payload.
- `lpcm` is essentially free (a pure `reinterpret`); both RLE and zstd trade speed for size, but for typical hypnogram sizes the absolute encode/decode times are still in the microseconds.

Timings are medians from BenchmarkTools `@benchmark` runs on a single host; absolute numbers will vary across machines, but relative ordering should be stable.
